    Diagnosis:   Hydraulic oil cooler defective.

  [exp_12] Oil level falls continuously due to a leak, eventually dropping below the threshold and low‑level alarms are triggered.

    Alarms:      Hyd_A_700205, Hyd_A_700206
    Diagnosis:   Leakage in hydraulics system

  [exp_13] The hydraulic pump motor fails spontaneously during operation, causing condition monitoring to trigger.
    Root causes: Hyd_Pump_Ok
    Alarms:      Hyd_A_700208
    Diagnosis:   Hydraulic pump motor fails

  [exp_14] A hydraulic leak develops. First, a warning for low oil level is issued. Hydraulic pressure then continuously drops until its threshold is also breached.  Both values eventually fall below limits, triggering multiple fault alarms.
    Root causes: Hyd_Level_Ok, Hyd_Pressure
    Alarms:      Hyd_A_700202, Hyd_A_700205, Hyd_A_700206
    Diagnosis:   Leakage in hydraulics system

  [exp_15] A developing  cooling issues causes oil temperature to rise above 70°C, triggering a temperature warning. Hydraulic pressure then begins to drop, while the temperature continues to rise. In some runs, the pressure drops first; in others, the oil temperature exceeds the critical 80°C threshold first. Eventually, both conditions breach their respective limits, triggering fault alarms. The operator acknowledges the errors and terminates the experiment.
    Root causes: Hyd_Temp_lt_70, Hyd_Temp_lt_80, Hyd_Pressure
    Alarms:      Hyd_A_700202, Hyd_A_700203, Hyd_A_700204
    Diagnosis:   Leakage in hydraulics system
    Diagnosis:   Hydraulic oil cooler defective.

  [exp_18] Due to Leakage the oil level begins to fall continuously, eventually leading to motor failure.
    Root causes: Hyd_Level_Ok, Hyd_Pump_Ok
    Alarms:      Hyd_A_700205, Hyd_A_700206, Hyd_A_700208
    Diagnosis:   Leakage in hydraulics system
    Diagnosis:   Hydraulic pump motor fails

  [exp_20] Contamination in the system causes the return filter to become progressively blocked, increasing back pressure. Eventually, this results in hydraulic motor failure.
    Root causes: Hyd_Filter_Ok, Hyd_Pump_Ok
    Alarms:      Hyd_A_700207, Hyd_A_700208
    Diagnosis:   Hydraulics flow obstructed by clogged filter.

  [exp_8] A leak causes hydraulic pressure to fall continuously; once pressure drops below the threshold (Hyd_Pressure < ca. 6600), the system detects a low‑pressure fault and raises an alarm.
    Root causes: Hyd_Pressure
    Alarms:      Hyd_A_700202
    Diagnosis:   Leakage in hydraulics system

  [exp_9] The defective pressure‑level sensor outputs random values outside the normal range—spiking both above and below the true pressure—until it falsely triggers a pressure fault alarm. Operator acknowledges the fault and terminates the experiment.
    Root causes: Hyd_Pressure
    Alarms:      Hyd_A_700202
    Diagnosis:   Hyd_Pressure Sensor is defective.
